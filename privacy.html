Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026 | Effective Date: September 5, 2026

Welcome to Catch the Stars ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are dedicated to protecting any information you provide while playing our game. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and how it is secured.

1. Information We Collect

When you register an account in Catch the Stars, we collect the following minimal information necessary to provide game functionality:

  • Email Address: Used solely as your account identifier for sign-in and profile restoration.
  • In-Game Name (IGN): A player-chosen display nickname shown on the public global leaderboard.
  • Password (Hashed): Stored securely using cryptographic hashing (SHA-256 / scrypt). We never store or have access to your plain-text password.
  • Game Performance Data: High score, star points caught, survival time, and total games played.

2. How Your Information is Used

We use the collected information exclusively for:

  • Authenticating your login sessions so you can play across devices and resume your progress.
  • Displaying real-player rankings, scores, and survival times on the global leaderboard.
  • Allowing you to update your In-Game Name upon request.
  • Preventing duplicate accounts and cheating.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in secure server environments with encrypted HTTPS connections. Password hashes cannot be decrypted. We adopt industry best practices to safeguard against unauthorized access or data exposure.

4. Publicly Visible Information

Only your In-Game Name (IGN), your high score, and your survival time appear on the public leaderboard. Your email address and password hash are completely private and never shown to other players.

5. Children's Privacy

Catch the Stars is a family-friendly casual arcade game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifying information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent.

6. Account Deletion & Data Inquiries

If you wish to delete your account, remove your leaderboard entry, or inquire about your stored data, please contact the developer via GitHub at github.com/trinobindu/Falling-Stars.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated Effective Date.
