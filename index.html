⭐ How to Play

  • 👆
    Click or tap falling stars before they reach the bottom of the game area.
  • ⏳
    Endless Survival Mode: No time limit! Stay alive and rack up points as long as you can.
  • 💔
    Don't miss 5 stars! If 5 stars escape past the bottom, the game ends.
  • 👤
    Sign in with Gmail: Log in with your Gmail to record and save your personal high score!
  • 🌟
    Star Types & Bonus Points:
    ⭐ Normal Star +1 pt
    🌟 Golden Star +3 pts
    💫 Supernova Star +5 pts
  • 📈
    Dynamic Speed: As your score increases, stars fall faster and appear more often!
  • ⌨️
    Keyboard Controls:

    Space Start/Play Again  |  P Pause/Resume  |  M Mute Audio
